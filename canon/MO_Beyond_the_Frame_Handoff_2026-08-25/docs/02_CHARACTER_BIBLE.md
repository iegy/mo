# Character Bible

# MO / مو

## Canon visual identity
Use the files under `media/01_original_mo_references/` as the model reference. Do not redesign Mo from later generated concept boards.

### Core silhouette
- large rounded rectangular monitor/screen head;
- single antenna with orange ball tip;
- compact torso;
- segmented flexible arms;
- simple mechanical hands/claws;
- wheel/tread base;
- soft industrial toy-like materials;
- cream/beige + sage/olive + soft orange accents;
- dark face screen.

### Core personality
- curious;
- kind;
- clever;
- easily stressed;
- sometimes stubborn;
- brave when it truly matters;
- unintentionally funny.

### Speech rule
Mo does not speak words.

### Screen expression library
Suggested expression family:
- neutral;
- happy;
- very happy;
- shocked;
- confused;
- suspicious;
- tired;
- annoyed;
- angry;
- scared;
- embarrassed;
- sad;
- idea;
- love/heart;
- alarm.

Exact graphical syntax can vary as long as it stays minimal and readable.

### Acting rule
Never rely on the screen expression alone. Emotion should combine:
- screen;
- head tilt;
- antenna;
- arm posture;
- body height/compression;
- wheel/tread motion;
- timing.

### Antenna acting
- curiosity: leans forward;
- shock: upright;
- sadness: droops slightly;
- speed: trails behind;
- fear: subtle shake;
- alert: quick upright snap.

### Movement personality
Mo is mechanical but animated with warmth.

Principle:
**Mechanical body — animated soul.**

Use anticipation, follow-through, subtle overshoot, and weight. Avoid making him look like an industrial machine executing rigid loops.

### Movement library target
- idle;
- look left/right;
- walk;
- fast walk;
- run;
- panic run;
- reverse;
- jump;
- fall;
- land;
- slide;
- spin;
- sudden stop;
- peek;
- climb;
- hang;
- point;
- wave;
- grab;
- push;
- pull;
- carry;
- drop;
- tap;
- knock;
- inspect;
- protect face;
- shrug;
- celebrate;
- freeze;
- sad idle;
- angry stomp;
- curious lean;
- nervous look;
- embarrassed;
- proud;
- exhausted.

### Comedy language
Comedy should come from timing and reactions. Example pattern: Mo sees a “DON'T PRESS” button, considers it, backs away, then his arm re-enters frame and presses it.

# PIX / بيكس

## Concept status
Personality and narrative role are approved. Exact final model is not fully locked.

## Core concept
PIX is a living fragment/pixel of warm orange light and energy, approximately hand-sized relative to Mo.

PIX is:
- curious;
- playful;
- impulsive;
- mischievous;
- loyal to Mo;
- knowledgeable about The Margin;
- frightened of The Blank;
- hiding part of the truth.

PIX does not speak normal dialogue.

### Communication
- chirps;
- digital pops;
- whistles;
- glow intensity;
- movement;
- temporary symbols/shapes such as arrow, heart, exclamation mark, question mark, star.

### Movement
PIX flies rather than walks.
- hover;
- dart;
- loop;
- hide behind Mo;
- enter small spaces;
- guide with light;
- fast directional bursts.

### Mo/PIX dynamic
- Mo thinks; PIX acts.
- PIX leads, Mo follows.
- PIX teases Mo.
- In danger, PIX protects Mo.
- Their silent communication should work across Arabic and English versions.
