# Visual & Interaction Bible

## 1. Approved visual direction

Primary reference:
`media/02_approved_visual_mockup/01_approved_interactive_comic_mockup.png`

The overall look is:
- colored cinematic comic/manga;
- soft polished 3D/illustrative rendering;
- warm cream and beige;
- muted sage/olive greens;
- gentle orange accents;
- muted grays and dark screen values;
- warm, whimsical, adventurous mood;
- family friendly;
- modern web presentation;
- manga speed lines and expressive panel shapes when appropriate;
- no harsh cyberpunk neon overload;
- no generic anime-human aesthetic imposed onto Mo.

## 2. Panels as storytelling

Panel layout is not a fixed grid.

Possible panel types:
- wide cinematic;
- standard rectangle;
- tall vertical slice;
- circular insert;
- floating panel;
- broken frame;
- full bleed.

### Calm scenes
- orderly panels;
- wide margins;
- gentle movement;
- stable composition.

### Danger scenes
- panels tilt;
- overlap;
- compress;
- break;
- disappear.

The page design itself should reflect emotional state.

## 3. The Blank visual language

Do not draw a conventional villain body.

Erasure sequence:
1. detail disappears;
2. texture disappears;
3. color disappears;
4. outlines disappear;
5. sound is removed;
6. movement is removed;
7. only pure white remains.

The Blank should feel unsettling because reality is missing, not because a monster is chasing the characters.

## 4. Broken Corner Mark

A recurring broken-corner visual symbol appears subtly in the visual language and can later become part of the series lore/brand.

## 5. Scroll modes

### Free Scroll
Normal page progress.

### Guided Scroll
Scroll progress drives a scene timeline.

### Pinned Scene
The viewport holds while internal scene action advances. Use sparingly.

## 6. Interaction rules

Every optional interaction must do at least one of:
- strengthen the scene;
- reveal personality;
- reward curiosity.

If it does none of those, remove it.

The story must complete with scroll only.

## 7. Soft choices

Occasional choice moments may branch into 10–30 second variants and then reconnect. Do not build a full branching narrative tree unless a future deliberate decision changes this rule.

## 8. Mobile

Mobile is not merely a cropped desktop version.

Important sequences should receive deliberate vertical staging, especially:
- frame breaking;
- falling between worlds;
- action/escape.

Do not rely on hover for required interaction.

## 9. Sound

Sound is optional and immediately muteable.

Use:
- ambient music;
- world ambience;
- mechanical motion;
- Mo beeps;
- PIX chirps;
- impacts;
- transitions.

The Blank's signature is the subtraction of sound rather than monster sound design.

## 10. Accessibility

The story must remain understandable with:
- sound off;
- reduced motion;
- keyboard navigation;
- narrow screen.

For reduced-motion mode use fades, cuts, and static poses instead of large camera movements.
