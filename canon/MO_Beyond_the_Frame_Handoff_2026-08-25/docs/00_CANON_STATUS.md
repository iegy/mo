## LOCKED / CANON

### Main character
- Official name: **Mo / مو**.
- Mo does not speak with words.
- Mo expresses himself through his screen face, symbols, body language, antenna movement, movement timing, and nonverbal electronic sounds.
- Visual identity: use the final canon files under `media/00_CANON_CHARACTER_DESIGNS/`.
- Core palette: warm cream/beige, sage/olive green, soft orange accents, muted gray/dark screen.
- Key design traits: rounded cream monitor head, dark emissive screen face, orange-tipped single antenna, compact sage/cream body, articulated arms/hands, tracked base, friendly soft-industrial materials.

### PIX
- PIX / بيكس is now visually locked.
- PIX is a small living fragment of light/energy, not a robot and not literal fire.
- Final shape language: warm orange luminous body, dark expressive face core, pale cream/yellow glow, fluid teardrop/orb silhouette, expressive shape changes, light trail.
- Use the final canon files under `media/00_CANON_CHARACTER_DESIGNS/`.

### Series identity
- Approved series name: **MO — Beyond the Frame / مو — خارج الإطار**.
- Format: **Interactive Cinematic Comic**.
- Structure: chapters; each chapter is released complete.
- Target chapter duration: **10–15 minutes**, longer with optional exploration.
- Audience: all ages, layered family-animation accessibility.
- Tone priority: **Comedy → Adventure → Mystery → Emotion → Action → Science Fiction**.
- Each adventure is self-contained but contributes clues to a larger ongoing mystery.

### Language
- Site is bilingual Arabic/English.
- Arabic dialogue is natural light Egyptian Arabic.
- English is adapted, not mechanically translated.
- Mo and PIX remain understandable without language.

### World
- Mo begins unaware that anything exists outside the frame.
- Multiple worlds exist, not only a coding/programming world.
- Worlds are bounded by **Frames**.
- Interstitial space: **The Margin / الهامش**.
- Long-term mysterious threat: **The Blank / الفراغ**.
- The Blank is not a black monster. It is visual absence: detail → texture → color → outline → sound → movement → white.

### Human characters
- Humans may appear in some worlds/chapters.

### Emotion
- Some chapters may contain genuinely sad or touching moments while preserving all-ages tone.

### Interaction philosophy
- Story first.
- Scroll is the primary storytelling control.
- Supplementary Tap/Click interactions are optional.
- Soft choices may change a short moment but reconnect to the main route.
- Story remains complete even if optional interactions are ignored.
