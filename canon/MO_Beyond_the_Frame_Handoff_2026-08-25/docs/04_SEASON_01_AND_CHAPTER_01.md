# Season 01 + Chapter 01

# Season One — Beyond the Frame / خارج الإطار

Recommended structure: 8 complete chapters.

## Chapter 01 — الخطأ الذي لم يكن خطأً
### The Error That Wasn't

Mo lives an ordinary day. A strange “error” produces PIX. PIX passes beyond the edge of the panel. Mo follows, discovers the existence of the frame, falls through worlds, lands in The Margin, and encounters The Blank for the first time.

## Chapter 02 — محطة آخر الليل
### The Last Stop

Mo and PIX discover a train inside The Margin. It stops briefly in impossible worlds and reaches a station not shown on its route. A mysterious human passenger appears to recognize Mo.

## Chapter 03 — المدينة التي نامت
### The Sleeping City

An entire city is frozen in time. Rain, traffic, and people hang motionless. Mo can still move. A human child can move too and waits for her frozen parents. The city is eventually restored, but a mark associated with The Blank is discovered.

## Chapter 04 — فوق السحاب
### Above the Clouds

A lighter comedic adventure on a flying island. Its inhabitants misinterpret Mo as something legendary. This chapter introduces a character who may later join the team.

## Chapter 05 — آخر لون
### The Last Color

The Blank begins removing color from an entire world. Colors disappear gradually until PIX's orange remains one of the last strong colors. Highly visual and emotional.

## Chapter 06 — مدينة الـMo
### The City of Mo

Mo reaches a city populated by many different versions of himself. Comedy turns into mystery when one copy remembers something Mo does not: a memory from before Mo's known life.

## Chapter 07 — الصفحة البيضاء
### The White Page

Mo enters a completely blank space and discovers that he can create pieces of reality. Creating things appears to have a cost connected to memory. PIX is terrified and tries to stop him.

## Chapter 08 — خارج الإطار
### Beyond the Frame

Mo returns home and notices that his original world contains repetitive loops and impossible rules. He begins to suspect it was designed to contain him. The Blank finally appears but does not attack. Instead, it opens a path.

# Chapter 01 Production Storyboard

Target duration: 11–13 minutes normal, 14–17 minutes with exploration.

## Scene 01 — صباح جديد
ID: `ch01-sc01-awakening`

- dark opening;
- thin lines form comic panels;
- city wakes up;
- camera approaches Mo;
- Mo begins his morning walk;
- optional tap makes Mo look toward the tap.

Mood: calm.

## Scene 02 — لا أخطاء اليوم
ID: `ch01-sc02-no-errors`

- sign reads **NO ERRORS TODAY** / **مفيش أخطاء النهارده**;
- Mo is pleased;
- one step later it becomes **1 ERROR DETECTED** / **تم اكتشاف خطأ واحد**;
- Mo reacts `-_-`.

Easter egg: tap the sign three times → **ARE YOU SURE? / متأكد؟** briefly appears.

This scene is recommended as the first real production prototype for walking + parallax + character reaction.

## Scene 03 — الطريق إلى العمل
ID: `ch01-sc03-city-walk`

Parallax exploration scene with optional interactions:
- drinks machine drops a cup and Mo misses it;
- drone comes close and Mo waves;
- locked box becomes a recurring visual gag.

Subtle Broken Corner hint appears.

## Scene 04 — Error 4047
ID: `ch01-sc04-error4047`

Mo repairs a malfunctioning unit multiple times.

Attempt 1 → fail.
Attempt 2 → fail.
Attempt 3 → fail.
Attempt 4 → Mo taps the machine lightly and it works.

Hidden signature on the device: **MH-4047**.

Then a small orange light escapes.

## Scene 05 — PIX
ID: `ch01-sc05-pix`

PIX appears. Mo tries to catch him. PIX mimics Mo's expressions and dodges taps. The relationship begins as silent visual comedy. PIX heads toward the panel edge.

## Scene 06 — الحافة
ID: `ch01-sc06-the-edge`

PIX passes directly beyond the panel boundary. Mo discovers the frame line, touches it, puts his hand through, pulls back in shock, then pushes the edge and feels it bend.

Mood shifts from curiosity to impossible mystery.

## Scene 07 — كسر الإطار
ID: `ch01-sc07-frame-break`

Signature project moment.

Scroll drives:
- Mo pushes;
- border bends;
- cracks form;
- distortion appears;
- frame tears/breaks;
- Mo opens the panel boundary like a door.

The effect should feel like paper + interface + reality, not ordinary glass.

Broken Corner Mark appears.

## Scene 08 — السقوط بين العوالم
ID: `ch01-sc08-fall`

Major vertical sequence.

Mo falls through glimpses of multiple worlds:
- neon/future city;
- vertical ocean;
- cable forest;
- pencil sketch world;
- mechanical desert;
- pure white space.

Comedy beat: Mo catches a panel edge, celebrates, then the edge tears off and he falls again.

Easter egg: a distant silhouette resembling another Mo appears briefly.

## Scene 09 — الهامش
ID: `ch01-sc09-margin`

Mo wakes in The Margin. Environment includes story fragments such as doors without walls, stairs without buildings, windows opening to other worlds, orphaned roads.

Optional interactions:
- door opens to sea;
- window glimpses a future world;
- staircase leads nowhere.

Easter egg: door marked **01** cannot be opened. PIX behaves as though he recognizes it but hides that fact.

## Scene 10 — شيء يتبعنا
ID: `ch01-sc10-followed`

The reader notices the environment losing detail before Mo does. The Blank's approach is visible as gradual absence. PIX freezes in fear.

## Scene 11 — The Blank
ID: `ch01-sc11-blank`

Half of a doorway disappears as if it were never drawn. PIX stops Mo from touching the blank edge and urgently directs him away.

Key implication: PIX knows what The Blank is.

## Scene 12 — اهرب!
ID: `ch01-sc12-escape`

Scroll speed influences Mo's apparent run speed but there is no fail state.

Beats:
- ground disappears;
- jump over missing bridge;
- falling panel pieces;
- PIX guides with light;
- first door opens onto a wall as a comedy beat;
- second door is the escape;
- Mo barely gets through before The Blank reaches him.

## Epilogue

Mo and PIX emerge into an unknown large city. Mo looks at PIX, the world, then toward the viewer. A tiny blank patch exists far away in the sky.

End card:
**CHAPTER 01 COMPLETE / نهاية الفصل الأول**

Teaser: a train passes through white space → Chapter 02: **محطة آخر الليل**.
